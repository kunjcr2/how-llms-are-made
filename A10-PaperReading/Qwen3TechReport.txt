--- Qwen 3 Technical Report - https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.09388

-- Intro
- dense as well as sparse MoE
- Multimodal
- 36T tokens
- Qwen2.5-VL used to scan text from images and PDFs to get more data. more from Math and Code versions of the data.
- 3 stage strategy: 30T tokens for foundation, further more for the reasoning in STEM, and last long context data for 4k->32k context.
- multi stage post training, for thinking and non-thinking. First, developed cot and then rl on maths and coding. 
- Lastly general RL for overall reasoning tasks. USed distillation for smaller models from bigger ones.

-- Architecture
- 6 dense models
- 2 MoE models
- Grouped Query Attention, SqiGLU, RoPE, RMSNorm, prenormalization. No QKV-bias, used QK-Norm.

--- Qwen 3.5 Technical Report

-- Intro
- Multimodal
- Linear attention (Gated Delta networks)
- Sparse moe - no shared experts
- 397B model - 17B active
- 262K context

-- Vision language Eval
- Works mostly well with benchmarks near GPT-5.2, Kimi K2 as well as Gemini-3 pro and in many case better.
- Post training gains some nice improvements over Qwen 3. RL environment is helping in scaling up.

-- Pretraining
- Power: Larger scale of Visual-text tokens compared to Qwen 3 especially filled with en/ch, multilingual, stem and reasoning data with strict filtering.
- Efficiency: MoE, Gated Deltanet + Gated Attention, MTP.
- Versatility: Natively multimodal with early text-vision fusion and expanded visual/Stem/video data. 


--- Qwen 3.5 Omni
- Architecture update from the Qwen 3.5 Model
- Nothing significant in terms of VLM Architecture, just some tweaks to make it more powerful.
